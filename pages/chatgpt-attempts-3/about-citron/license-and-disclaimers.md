# License and Disclaimers

Citron uses the **Citron Modpack License (CML) v1.0**. Read the [full licence in the repository](https://github.com/PrecisionZero/citron/blob/main/LICENSE) before reusing or redistributing any part of the pack.

## What the licence covers

The CML applies to Citron's own content, including its pack structure, configuration files, logos, banners, and custom backgrounds. It permits personal use and redistribution of Citron's original content under its stated terms, with specific restrictions on reusing Citron branding.

**Third-party mods are not relicensed by Citron.** Each mod remains under its own licence. Follow those terms and retain any required attribution.

The full licence text takes precedence over this summary.

## Server rules

Citron is designed for client-side use, but not every server allows every mod or feature. Check the rules of the server you play on and ask staff if you're unsure. Server staff have the final say on what is permitted.

## Use at your own risk

Citron is provided as-is, without a warranty. Compatibility and behaviour can change between builds, and additional mods or configuration changes can cause issues. Back up important files before updating or modifying an instance.

Citron is not an official Minecraft product and is not associated with or endorsed by Mojang Studios or Microsoft.

Only download Citron from its [official Modrinth project](https://modrinth.com/modpack/citron) or the [official repository](https://github.com/PrecisionZero/citron).
