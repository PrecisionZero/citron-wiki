# Philosophy

There are plenty of Minecraft modpacks focused on performance, and plenty focused on quality-of-life features. Citron is my attempt to bring both together into one comfortable experience.

A purely performance-focused pack isn't what everyone wants. Some players have capable hardware and want more than higher FPS; they want Minecraft to feel more convenient, clean, and enjoyable.

At the same time, adding too many features can create unnecessary complexity. There isn't a perfect modpack for everyone. It depends on what you want from the game and the hardware you're using.

## Integrated Utility

The idea is to bring useful features together with settings chosen to work well as a pack. Players shouldn't have to spend hours searching for everyday features, installing individual mods, and configuring everything themselves. **The boring stuff should already be done.**

## Features over Mods

Mods can be updated, replaced, abandoned, or become unnecessary. What matters is the feature a mod provides, not its popularity or how many features it advertises.

If another mod provides the same feature more reliably or with less unnecessary complexity, it's worth considering. I believe in **less is more**: if something adds complexity without providing enough value, it's bloat.

That's the direction behind Citron: performance, convenience, and a Vanilla-respecting experience for players with reasonably capable hardware.
