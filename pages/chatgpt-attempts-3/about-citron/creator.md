# Creator

### Precision. In every way.

Citron is developed and maintained by **PrecisionZero**.

Starting out with Minecraft utility resource packs, PrecisionZero built a foundation around practical tools that make the game more convenient. Citron carries that same approach into a modpack: useful features, deliberate configuration, and no additions just for the sake of making the project look bigger.

The goal is to build a smooth, convenient, clean, and reliable experience while staying true to Vanilla Minecraft.

For the project itself, visit the [Citron repository](https://github.com/PrecisionZero/citron) or [Citron on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/modpack/citron).
