# Shulker Box Previews

Previewing a shulker box helps you check its contents without placing it in the world.

## How to use it

Hover over a shulker box in your inventory or another supported item view and follow the tooltip behaviour for your installed version.

## Provider

The development record associates this feature with [Shulker Box Tooltip](https://modrinth.com/mod/shulkerboxtooltip).

## Version record

First recorded in **1.0.0-dev.1**. The precise display and controls may vary by mod version.
