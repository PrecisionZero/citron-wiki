# Visual Comfort Options

This feature groups optional visual controls recorded for Citron, including presentation details such as overlays and fog. The exact options depend on the provider version.

## How to use it

Look through the relevant mod settings or video options in your build. Not every option is guaranteed to be present in every Citron version.

## Provider

The development record associates this feature group with [BactroMod](https://modrinth.com/mod/bactromod).
