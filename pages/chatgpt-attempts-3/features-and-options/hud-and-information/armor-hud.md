# Armor HUD

An armor HUD shows armor information while you play, so you do not have to open your inventory just to check it.

## How to use it

Look for armor indicators on the HUD. Check the provider's settings if you need to change their position or appearance.

## Provider

The development record associates this feature with [uku's Armor HUD](https://modrinth.com/mod/ukus-armor-hud).
