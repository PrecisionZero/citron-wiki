# Creator

### Precision. In every way.

Citron is developed and maintained by **PrecisionZero**. He started out creating utility-focused Minecraft resource packs and gradually built his place in the community through his work.

Although he may not be widely known, there's a chance you've already used one of his packs without realising it.

Check out his [page](https://precisionzero.carrd.co/) for more about his work and projects.
