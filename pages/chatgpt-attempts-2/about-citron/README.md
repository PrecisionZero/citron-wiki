# About Citron

Citron is a Minecraft modpack focused on performance, quality of life, and convenience. The goal is to make Minecraft more comfortable to play without turning it into another game.

It's meant for players who want useful features ready to go and have reasonably capable hardware. Citron isn't meant to be the smallest performance-only pack; if that's what you're looking for, Adrenaline may be a better fit.
