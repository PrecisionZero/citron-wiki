# Development and Release Cycle

Citron's development follows its Adrenaline foundation while maintaining its own selection of features, configurations, and quality-of-life improvements.

When Adrenaline supports a new Minecraft version or publishes a patch, Citron may follow after the relevant changes have been reviewed and tested. Timing is not guaranteed: a dependency, compatibility issue, or Citron-specific change may delay a build.

Development builds can change more frequently than stable releases. Check each version page for its Minecraft version, loader, build identifier, and recorded changes. Do not assume a development build is equivalent to a final release.
