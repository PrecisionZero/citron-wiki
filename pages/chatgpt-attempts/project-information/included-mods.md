# Included Mods

Citron documents its features by the benefit they provide, rather than treating a fixed mod list as the identity of the pack. However, knowing which third-party projects have been used is useful for transparency and credits.

The list below is taken from the repository's development history. It records mods added during development; it is **not a verified manifest of the latest downloadable build**. Check the release file and version notes before assuming a mod is still included.

## Recorded in the initial development build

**Performance and rendering**

* BadOptimizations
* Clumps
* Iris Shaders
* Sodium Extra
* Stfu
* Continuity

**Quality of life and interface**

* AppleSkin
* BactroMod
* Better Tab
* BetterF3
* Controlling
* Durability Plus
* Searchables
* Shulker Box Tooltip
* uku's Armor HUD
* Zoomify

**Libraries and configuration**

* ukulib
* YetAnotherConfigLib (YACL)

## Added in later development builds

* **Status Effect Timer** — recorded in `1.0.0-dev.2`.
* **Mouse Tweaks** and **LambDynamicLights** — recorded in `1.0.0-dev.3`.
* **FancyMenu**, **Konkrete**, and **Melody** — recorded in `1.0.0-dev.5` as part of the custom-menu work.

The development record also notes that **Simple Menu** was added in `dev.4` and removed in `dev.5` when the menu implementation changed.

For the source of these records, see the [Citron development history](https://github.com/PrecisionZero/citron/blob/main/Developer/Development_stage.md). Each mod remains the property of its respective author and is subject to its own licence.
