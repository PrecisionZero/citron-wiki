# Performance

Citron includes performance improvements, but performance is only one part of its purpose. It also includes quality-of-life features and carefully selected configurations.

Actual results depend on your hardware, Minecraft version, world, settings, and other software running on your computer. A modpack cannot guarantee a specific FPS or eliminate every stutter.

## Understanding performance changes

Document each included performance feature by explaining what it changes, what kind of benefit it may provide, and which mod supplies it. Avoid promising a fixed FPS increase unless it has been measured under clearly described test conditions.

For a specific build, refer to its [version page](../../getting-started/versions/index.md) and the official release notes.
