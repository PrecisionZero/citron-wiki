# Quality of Life

Quality-of-life features make everyday Minecraft actions more convenient without trying to turn the game into something else.

Citron's **Features over Mods** approach means the feature matters more than the name or popularity of a mod. The pack may replace a mod if another option provides the same benefit more reliably or with less unnecessary complexity.

The Feature Index lists the documented player-facing features and identifies the mod associated with each one. Since Citron is in development, check your exact version before relying on a feature or keybind.
