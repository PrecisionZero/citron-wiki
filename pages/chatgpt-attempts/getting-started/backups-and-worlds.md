# Backups and Worlds

Your worlds and personal changes are valuable. Make a backup before updating Citron, changing its loader or Minecraft version, or experimenting with additional mods.

## What to back up

* Your Minecraft worlds (the `saves` folder for the relevant instance).
* Configurations you have personally changed.
* Screenshots, resource packs, shader packs, or other files you want to keep.

## Safer updates

If possible, keep the previous instance until the new one launches successfully and your worlds load correctly. Do not assume that a world saved on a newer Minecraft version can be opened safely by an older version.

The exact folder location depends on your launcher and instance settings. Use the launcher's instance-folder or open-folder option rather than guessing the path.
