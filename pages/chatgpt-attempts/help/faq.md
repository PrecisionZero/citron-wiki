# FAQ

## Is Citron client-side?

Citron is designed to run on the player's client, so a server generally does not need Citron installed. Individual servers may still restrict particular mods or features; check their rules before joining.

## Is Citron a performance-only modpack?

No. Citron combines performance improvements with quality-of-life and convenience features. If you want a pack focused more narrowly on performance, Citron recommends checking out Adrenaline.

## Can I add my own mods?

Yes, but extra mods can introduce conflicts or change performance. Back up your instance and add mods one at a time. See Adding Extra Mods.

## Why are some buttons missing from the main menu?

Citron uses a custom menu. Switch to **Default Menu** to access buttons that are not shown on the Citron menu, including Language and Accessibility in the documented development build.

## Why did a setting change after an update?

An update may change defaults or replace configuration files. Check the relevant version notes and restore your preferences if needed.

## Where can I find what changed in a build?

Check the [Citron Versions](../../getting-started-1/versions/index.md) section. Features and configurations may change between development builds.

## Can I use Citron on multiplayer servers?

Citron is client-side, but server rules still apply. Check each server's policies for its rules on client-side mods and gameplay features.
