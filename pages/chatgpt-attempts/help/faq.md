# FAQ

## Is Citron client-side?

Citron is designed to run on the player's client, so a server generally does not need Citron installed. Individual servers may still restrict particular mods or features; check their rules.

## Is Citron a performance-only modpack?

No. Citron combines performance improvements with quality-of-life and convenience features. If you want a pack focused purely on performance, the project recommends looking at Adrenaline.

## Can I add my own mods?

You can customise your instance, but extra mods may introduce conflicts or change performance. Back up your instance and add mods one at a time.

## Why did a setting change after an update?

An update may change configuration defaults or replace configuration files. Check the relevant version notes and restore your personal preferences if needed.

## Where can I find what changed in a build?

Check the [Citron Versions](../../getting-started/versions/index.md) section. Feature availability can vary by build.
