# Configurations and Settings

Citron ships with selected configurations intended to make its included mods work well together. Defaults may change between builds as mods and compatibility requirements change.

You can adjust settings to suit your preferences, but changing configurations may affect performance, visuals, or compatibility. When troubleshooting, test with the original Citron settings first.

Before updating, keep a copy of personal configuration changes. After an update, check the settings you rely on and restore your preferences if they were reset or changed.

* [Changed Options and Menu](../../features/changed-options-and-menu.md) — custom menu behaviour and missing buttons.
* [Resetting Settings](resetting-settings.md) — safely restore settings without deleting unrelated files.

When documenting a specific option, include its location, default value for the relevant build, and what changing it does. Do not assume that every option exists in every version.
