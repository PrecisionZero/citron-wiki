# Modrinth App — Disabling Mods

1. Open Modrinth and go to **Library**.
2. Select your Citron instance and open its content list.
3. Find the mod you want to disable.
4. If the app provides an enable/disable control, use it and restart the game.
5. If there is no toggle, avoid deleting bundled files unless you understand their dependencies.

> Do not disable required libraries or dependencies unless you know what relies on them. Removing a bundled mod may make Citron behave differently or fail to launch.

If you're removing a mod you added yourself, start with that addition. To return to a known-good setup, install a fresh Citron instance and restore your backed-up personal files.
