# CurseForge App — Disabling Mods

1. Open CurseForge and go to **Minecraft → My Modpacks**.
2. Select your Citron profile and open its mod or content list.
3. Find the mod you want to disable. If the app provides an enable/disable control, use it and restart the instance.
4. If no toggle is available, avoid deleting bundled files unless you know whether the mod is required.

> Disabling a bundled mod can break other mods or remove a feature Citron expects. Back up your instance first, and remove your own additions before changing Citron's bundled content.

If the game stops launching after a change, undo the last change or test a clean Citron profile.
