# How to Add Shaders

Check the feature list for your Citron build before following these steps, as shader support can change between versions.

## Install a shader pack

1. Launch Citron and open **Options**.
2. Open **Video Settings** and look for **Shader Packs**.
3. Download a shader pack that supports your Minecraft version and the shader loader in your Citron build, from a source you trust.
4. Add the shader ZIP through the Shader Packs screen or its folder-opening option.
5. Select the shader and apply it.

## If shaders are missing or don't work

* Confirm that a compatible shader loader is included and working.
* Check that the shader supports your Minecraft version.
* Disable other shaders and extra mods while testing.
* If the game crashes or performance drops too much, disable the shader and launch Citron again.

Shaders can be demanding even with performance optimisations. Results depend on your GPU, shader settings, world, and render distance.
